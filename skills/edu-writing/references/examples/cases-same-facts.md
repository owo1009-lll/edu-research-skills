# 跨案例差异承担解释功能

本项目原创构造示例；不是原文引文，不是留出任务输出。共同来源 N08 三个 Findings 案例、Discussion 的 RQ2 和 Implications、Conclusion 的样本限制。两版都使用同一事实：Mary 的新课程、Zoe 的教学竞赛、Peggy 的博士研究；描述符的具体用途；专业目标、理解与应用的联系；只选三位成功参与者；仍有适配困难。

## Accurate but plain

Three university English teachers used China's Standards of English Language Ability (CSE) for different professional purposes. Mary used its descriptors to develop objectives for a tourism course, Zoe to revise objectives in a teaching-competition lesson, and Peggy to identify proficiency levels for her doctoral study. The accounts linked the teachers' professional goals, growing understanding of the CSE, and attempts to apply it through discussions and consultation. They therefore illustrate several ways in which a professional-development program can support engagement with a new standards framework. Applying the descriptors still posed difficulties, including adapting them to the tourism course. The authors interpret these experiences in terms of teachers connecting themselves, a new tool and professional goals. Since the study selected three successful participants from nine volunteers, the account does not show how the other teachers responded or establish the program's overall effectiveness (Zhang & Peng, 2024).

## Accurate and explanatory

The three cases show that the same standards framework became useful through different professional problems. Mary needed concrete objectives for a tourism course, Zoe for a teaching-competition lesson, while Peggy needed proficiency levels for her doctoral study. CSE descriptors acquired a practical role as each teacher connected greater understanding of the framework to a particular goal through discussions and consultation. The contribution is thus more specific than a claim that professional development increased engagement: these accounts illustrate how teachers worked out what a new tool could do for work they already needed to accomplish. The connection remained incomplete; Mary still needed to adapt general descriptors to tourism. This leaves application as continuing work rather than a final stage automatically reached after acquiring knowledge. Because the three teachers were selected as successful participants from nine volunteers, the cases develop a process account but cannot explain all responses to the program or establish its overall effectiveness (Zhang & Peng, 2024).

## 改进与边界

- 基础版已清楚交代用途、专业目标、支持方式和选择限制，未删去必要证据。
- 第二版让不同目的成为共同过程的证据：同一个框架通过不同的实际问题获得用途。没有把差异简单写成三份案例摘要。
- Mary's adaptation difficulty changes the explanation: application is ongoing work, not a completed stage. This is the project's inference from a reported difficulty, not an experimentally verified sequence.
- 两版均不把三例成功推成全体教师效果；不声称职业成果由项目造成，也不把理论术语本身计为改进。
