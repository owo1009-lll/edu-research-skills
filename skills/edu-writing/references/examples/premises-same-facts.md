# 让规范性论证说明判断依据

本项目原创开发示例，非原文引文、非留出输出。共同来源 N05 Findings Themes 2、4、5 与 Discussion 的 Ethics of Differentiation。两版都使用26位教师的自报公平取向、支持分配、先进学生受到较少针对支持、遗憾和有限时间。增加资源能否缓解矛盾是两版共有的项目建议，不是论文检验结果。

## Accurate but plain

Interviews with 26 kindergarten teachers described a tension between supporting children who entered behind and challenging those who entered ahead. Teachers framed extra attention to lower-skilled children as fair, while directing much whole-group instruction toward the middle. Advanced children often received less targeted support, a pattern that some teachers regretted. These accounts raise questions about how kindergarten should balance closing readiness gaps and supporting each child's further learning. Both goals have educational value, but teachers described making choices under limited time. Additional support could help teachers pursue both aims, although the interviews do not establish the effects of greater capacity. The study therefore supports an explicit discussion of priorities in differentiation. It does not measure the consequences of particular allocations for children's learning, so the fairness of those allocations cannot be settled by treating teachers' accounts as evidence of an optimal distribution (Little & Rowan, 2026).

## Accurate and explanatory

Calling an allocation fair does not yet specify which educational aim it serves. In interviews with 26 kindergarten teachers, extra attention to children who entered behind was justified as fairness, while whole-group instruction largely addressed the middle. That judgment gave catching up priority under limited time; it left advanced children with less targeted support, which some teachers regretted. The question is what counts as adequate support when closing gaps and extending every child's learning place competing demands on available attention. Increasing support could ease the choice, so this is not an inevitable opposition between two goals. Yet the interviews cannot show how much extra capacity would help, or what children gained or lost from these allocations. They identify the grounds of a practical judgment without establishing its optimality. A defensible policy argument needs both an explicit priority and evidence about its consequences (Little & Rowan, 2026).

## 改进与边界

- 两版事实相同，也都承认两个目标的教育价值和资源可能改变选择条件。
- 第二版将“公平”的判断拆成目标、约束与后果要求，让读者可以判断论证依据。
- “两个目标必然冲突”不是前提；增加容量的可能性限制了这项推论。
- 不将教师遗憾写成先进学生已被测量的学习损失，不替作者或用户决定最优分配。
- 这是有实证材料支持的问题建构与规范性论证示例，不是政策效果复现。

Little, M., & Rowan, R. (2026). Teaching toward equity: Kindergarten teachers' responses to readiness variation and preschool skill gaps. *Early Childhood Education Journal*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10643-026-02310-9
