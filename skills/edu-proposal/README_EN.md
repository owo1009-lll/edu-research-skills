# `edu-proposal` Skill

[中文说明](README.md)

Drafts and checks Chinese grant applications and opening reports, from topic and argument to the anonymous review sheet.

## What To Use It For

- Applications or review sheets for the National Education Science Planning, the National Social Science Fund, Ministry of Education and provincial projects
- Opening reports for theses or projects

## Typical Requests

- "Draft an application on this year's National Education Science Planning review sheet"
- "Improve my rationale and innovation sections"

## What You Need To Provide

- Topic idea, existing materials and prior work, target fund and project type; this year's call or template if available

## Outputs

- The application text (Markdown and Word), argument map, evidence table, word-count and anonymity checks, and items for the applicant to confirm

## Runtime and Dependencies

- Python 3.9 or later; python-docx to read .docx

## Boundaries

- Structure and limits follow the current official call; the research basis reports only real work; promised outputs are binding at completion for the National Education Science Planning

## Related Skills

- edu-research (design), edu-review (reviewer's view), edu-writing (reference verification)

## Status

- Beta: National Education Science Planning rules checked against the 2026 template; tried on a synthetic topic
