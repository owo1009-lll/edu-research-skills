## What makes the question worth asking

The opening moves through interactive explanation, explanation to an absent peer, and oral versus written explanation (pp. 1–4). Each narrowing changes the activity under investigation. The resulting question concerns whether social cues can improve the written activity, rather than whether explanation is generally valuable. The potential educational use is also concrete: writing is readily implementable, so improving this particular activity matters.

The strongest theoretical passage gives the learner actions: select information, organize it, connect it to existing knowledge, elaborate it and notice gaps (p. 2). Theory supplies a process that the reader can imagine. A later passage specifies two routes from an imagined audience: adapting the explanation to that audience and feeling related to it (p. 3). These routes explain why both explanation content and motivational experience are measured. The article does not merely introduce a theory name before listing variables.

The design then makes the comparison intelligible. After studying an immunology text, 137 university students were randomized to oral explanation, a standard written explanation, a simulated messenger explanation or retrieval. Figure 1 makes the intended social cues visible: a named fictitious peer, a profile picture and a request for an explanation. This is a simulated recipient, not evidence of a responsive conversation. Distinguishing that action matters to the interpretation.

## Sentence and paragraph analysis

The first paragraph under learning by explaining to fictitious peers, p. 2, contains six sentences. The following is an original functional analysis, not a reproduction of the paragraph.

| Sentence | What it adds | How the language carries the argument |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Places explanation within generative learning | The activity is the subject; the goal is meaningful learning, not a vague claim about importance. |
| 2 | Specifies selecting and organizing information | A general theoretical statement ends in actions the learner must perform. This supplies the content behind the category introduced in sentence 1. |
| 3 | Adds connection to prior knowledge | The learner remains the actor. The temporal progression adds a different operation instead of restating organization. |
| 4 | Links that connection to details beyond the supplied material | The prior operation becomes the starting point for a new consequence. Examples and elaboration can subsequently be recognized in the measures. |
| 5 | Adds detection of incomplete understanding | The paragraph broadens from constructing an explanation to monitoring it; the new sentence earns the metacognitive part of the theory. |
| 6 | Connects this process to monitoring accuracy and related learning activities | Literature supports the proposed consequence and prepares the later measurement, rather than forming a separate list of studies. |

The reusable action is to unpack a theoretical relation into the activities that give it meaning. Its six-sentence form is not a template. Some causal language here expresses the theoretical account; it is not a result established by this experiment.

At p. 3, the two proposed routes from social presence use an imagined audience as the common starting point. One route ends in elaboration; the other in enjoyment and investment. The distinction makes two kinds of evidence necessary. Simply replacing the process verbs with abstract nouns such as facilitation or enhancement would remove the explanation even if the theory citations remained.

The paragraph beginning the recommendation for better explanations on p. 14 develops a different task:

| Move in sentence order | Added reasoning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Selects explanation quality as the next research problem, drawing on the observed outputs. |
| 2 | Proposes that learners may need support with challenging content. This is a rationale, not a measured cause of the null result. |
| 3–4 | Introduces a specific prior feedback intervention and the outcome reported in that study. The literature supplies an actionable example. |
| 5 | Adds training and prompts from the self-explanation literature as distinguishable support approaches. |
| 6 | Makes transfer to explanations for fictitious others an open question. This condition belongs beside that proposed extension. |

The authors' recommendation that future research should investigate support is a defensible research position. It need not be changed into a sequence of hesitant suggestions. The narrower repair concerns which evidence establishes the proposed process.

## How the whole article advances—and where it repeats

| Location | Contribution to the central question |
|---|---|
| pp. 1–4, introduction | Moves from a useful activity to a modality difference and then a proposed social account. The comparison has a reason beyond a local literature shortage. |
| pp. 4–10, present study and methods | Separates three explanation conditions from retrieval; identifies knowledge, monitoring, experience and output measures. The concrete chat and sample instructions make the manipulation inspectable. |
| pp. 10–13, results | Reports learning outcomes before experience and output differences. The question is not silently replaced by whichever secondary result is significant. |
| pp. 13–14, discussion | Acknowledges the absent predicted learning pattern, considers prior knowledge and explanation quality, and develops a support-oriented research direction. Motivation supplies an additional finding, not a substitute achievement outcome. |
| pp. 14–15, limitations and conclusion | Examines measurement and manipulation issues. However, the absent learning advantage and difficult-material explanation recur; these passages could be consolidated while preserving the evidence that complicates the interpretation. |

Tables 1–2 carry detailed measurements and associations. The prose is most useful when it explains the comparison or the relation between findings, rather than repeating each table entry. The discussion's reminder of the main result is necessary; a second full account of the same proposed difficulty explanation is less useful unless it adds new evidence.

## Findings and qualifications that must survive an edit

- The frequentist learning comparisons were not significant. Reported BF01 values were 9.43 for text-based and 128.04 for inference questions. The authors used additional Bayesian evidence, so describing the article as relying only on nonsignificance would be inaccurate. The models and prior specification were not independently audited here; these values do not license saying a null hypothesis has been proven true.
- All three explanation conditions had higher reported enjoyment and interest than retrieval. For effort, the significant post-hoc differences from retrieval were the two written conditions; the oral comparison was not significant (pp. 12–13). Preserve this distinction when summarizing the broader abstract wording.
- Chat explanations contained more personal references, but self-rated social presence did not differ significantly among explanation conditions (p. 10; Table 1). An output indicator and a self-report are not interchangeable. The experiment manipulated the presentation of social cues; its findings do not cleanly isolate the effect of an established increase in experienced presence.
- The authors report low knowledge-test homogeneity and discuss the breadth of the short tests (pp. 7, 15). This is relevant to what the outcome measures capture; it does not erase the observed experience differences.
- The exploratory enjoyment mediation on p. 14 is a reported analysis, not a preregistered confirmation of the proposed mechanism. A nonsignificant total comparison alone does not disqualify an indirect-effect analysis, but mediator interpretation still requires its own assumptions and model detail.

Two source issues remain visibly unresolved. Under the interest measure, p. 8 repeats the description and example used for enjoyment; the separate items require checking. The elaboration result on p. 13 prints F(2,100) = 10.35, p = .034 and partial eta-squared = .07 together. These exact printed values were visually verified; no replacement value has been invented. The reported switch from preregistered contrasts to MANCOVA after inspecting the descriptive pattern is also retained as an analysis change, not treated as universally preferable statistical practice.

## Original English same-fact editing example

These three paragraphs are newly authored development illustrations, **not quotations, observed skill failures, or controlled evaluation outputs**. The retained facts are the randomized four-condition study, its two knowledge outcomes and reported Bayesian evidence, the experience findings, and the disagreement between the presence indicators (pp. 6, 10–13).

**Before: correct elements, weak progression.**

> The study included 137 university students and four conditions: oral explanation, standard written explanation, messenger explanation, and retrieval. There were no significant condition differences on the two knowledge tests, and the reported Bayes factors favored the null models. Another finding was that all explanation conditions received higher enjoyment and interest ratings than retrieval. The two written conditions also received higher effort ratings than retrieval. Another issue concerns social presence. Messenger explanations contained more personal references than the other explanations, but the self-reported presence ratings did not differ significantly. Therefore, the learning outcomes, the experience ratings, and the social-presence indicators should all be considered in interpreting the findings.

**After: organize around the contribution of the comparison.**

> In this randomized study of 137 university students, explaining changed the reported learning experience without producing the predicted knowledge advantage over retrieval. All three explanation conditions received higher enjoyment and interest ratings, and the two written conditions received higher effort ratings. Neither knowledge test showed a significant condition difference; the reported Bayes factors favored the null models. The messenger condition also clarifies a measurement issue: it increased personal references relative to the other explanation conditions, while self-rated presence did not differ significantly. Thus, a more visibly interpersonal explanation did not establish a corresponding increase in experienced presence.

**Compressed: retain the argument, not every design detail.**

> Explaining improved reported enjoyment and interest relative to retrieval, and the two written conditions also increased reported effort. The predicted knowledge advantage was absent from the frequentist comparisons, with reported Bayesian evidence favoring the null models. Messenger explanations contained more personal references, but self-rated presence did not differ significantly. The study therefore distinguishes a changed learning experience and interpersonal wording from demonstrated gains in knowledge or experienced presence.

The revision replaces the repeated announcement of another result with the substantive contrast among outcomes. Compression removes the sample size and condition inventory because this is a discussion paragraph following methods; it retains both kinds of null evidence and the particular measurement tension. Neither edit invents an effective support intervention or a cause of the absent learning difference. The authors' support-oriented research recommendation can follow with its literature rationale, rather than being deleted or represented as an intervention already tested here.

## Relation to existing guidance

This source extends the comparison between Q-N07's theory-to-activity rationale and Q-N12's explanation of the task distinction. Its specific contribution is to follow the proposed process through manipulation, indicator, experience and outcome, while keeping a substantive finding when the predicted learning advantage is absent. Existing quantitative guidance already permits that work. The present note supplies development evidence and an English illustration; it does not establish that an additional rule improves current outputs, and does not restore the unadopted Q-C01 addition by assumption.
