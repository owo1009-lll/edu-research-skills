# M-N22 — A screening counterexample with a useful description of reflective practice

## Identity, reading and selection decision

Sánchez-Jiménez, Marta, María Maravé-Vivas, Jesús Gil-Gómez, and Celina Salvador-Garcia (2025). *Building pre-service teachers’ resilience through Service-Learning: an explanatory sequential mixed methods study*. Frontiers in Psychology, 16, 1568476. DOI: [10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1568476](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1568476). [Public publisher text](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1568476/full). The PDF states CC BY licensing.

All 11 PDF pages were read, including Tables 1–4, Figure 1, declarations and references. Actual page images inspected: 1, 3–9. Reading SHA-256: `ac53749998aea9e7a834844fbf142f90d2da0b3aa82db26c020eaa7936e57ea1`. Source and image hashes are in [reading provenance](../reading-mn22-mn23-assets.json). Full journals, participant data, analysis code and cited originals were not read. The paper's declaration that no generative AI was used is recorded as the authors' statement, not independently verified authorship evidence.

**Decision: retain as a completed English screening counterexample, separate from the empirical-exemplar count.** The authors explicitly attempt construct-level integration, so this is not simply a case of unrelated numbers and quotations. However, the displayed connection and ensuing interpretation do not adequately show how qualitative variation explains the quantitative pattern. The article also conditions its qualitative inquiry on statistical significance. These features make it unsuitable as a positive model of explanatory integration. Selected local description can still be useful without endorsing the whole argument.

## What the integration actually does

The programme involved 54 students; 31 supplied usable paired questionnaires. The report compares one group's pre/post scores and interprets reflective journals through predefined resilience categories. Table 4 links a single overall significance statement to categories and quotation identifiers. It does not display the component estimates beside the corresponding accounts, nor explain which journal writers contributed paired scores.

This is alignment around a construct, but the alignment alone cannot establish a mechanism of score change. The distinction matters editorially: adding a sentence that says two sources complement one another would not fill the missing inferential step. A stronger account would need to show what an actual experience explains about the measured pattern, using the evidence the project possesses. An editor must not invent participant matching, disagreement analysis or a later interview phase to make that account appear complete.

The authors' design description and Figure 1 make significance a condition for the second analysis. That is a counterexample for this corpus. An unchanged, uncertain or contradictory quantitative result can also raise a useful qualitative question. This does not require giving every strand equal weight or making all mixed research follow the same order.

## A local passage worth retaining

The final five sentences of the programme-organization paragraph on p. 5 describe reflection more clearly than the article's repeated assertions of comprehensive development:

| Sentence | Work performed |
|---|---|
| 1 | Identifies the reflective record and its individual component. |
| 2 | Adds feedback during review and after sessions, although calling it effective anticipates an evaluation. |
| 3 | Names the people who discussed the teaching activity. |
| 4 | Gives that discussion a purpose: identifying problems and planning improvements. |
| 5 | Explains why partners' experience adds a different perspective. |

The last three sentences make an educational arrangement intelligible. They say who contributes what and what the discussion is intended to change. That information can survive compression, while a general adjective about effectiveness can be removed unless separately supported. The transferable editorial action is to preserve the relationship among participants, knowledge and purpose.

At sentence level, teachers, partner staff and students are more informative subjects than repeated references to development, competencies or transformation. Naming those actors does not require turning a methods passage into a narrative scene. It gives an abstract term such as reflection a practical referent. The distinction between an intended improvement and a measured result should remain visible.

## Whole-article and conceptual judgment

The article begins with a broad societal framing, accumulates proposed service-learning benefits, defines resilience, presents the programme and then organizes interpretation around the resilience components. The programme description is the clearest bridge between a theoretical aim and something students actually do. The broad opening and repeated claims of comprehensive development add less to that bridge.

A revision should preserve the authors' educational commitment to community partnership. It need not repeat the entire societal framing to express that position. Nor should it convert an affirmative commitment into evidence that all listed benefits occurred. The findings must carry their own evidential burden.

Conceptual continuity also needs attention. Table 1 defines tolerance in terms of being able to express one's differences safely; the conclusion emphasizes tolerance of others' differences. These are related educational concerns, but they are not interchangeable definitions. A polished sentence must not quietly change the construct being interpreted.

The quotation analysis sometimes expands a participant's account into several outcomes without showing the intervening reasoning. In particular, a changed understanding of disability should not automatically become evidence of emotional recovery. The participant's revised view can be analytically valuable in its own terms. Protecting that value requires a more exact interpretation, not a uniformly weaker tone.

## Original same-fact English editing illustration

These are newly written descriptions based on the reported reflection arrangement, not source quotations or model-evaluation outputs. They illustrate a local improvement in specificity rather than repair the missing integration.

**Before.**

> Students took part in post-session reflection. Teachers took part too. Staff from the partner organizations also participated. They discussed students' teaching and the problems that had arisen. The aim was to identify improvements for later sessions. Partner staff contributed experience of working with the children. Reflection was an important part of the programme.

**After.**

> After sessions, students discussed their teaching with teachers and partner staff to identify problems and plan improvements. Partners brought experience of working with the children, giving the discussion an additional practical perspective.

**Compressed.**

> Students, teachers and partner staff reviewed teaching problems after sessions and planned improvements, drawing on partners' experience with the children.

The edit removes the generic closing endorsement while retaining the participants, timing, object, intended next action and partners' contribution. It does not add a measured benefit or turn a reflective record into independent observation of improved practice.

## Reporting issues retained

On p. 6, the prose reports `t(31) = −07796`; Table 3 lists the overall paired difference as −0.07796, t = −2.324 and df = 30. These were checked in the page image. The note records a document inconsistency without reconstructing the analysis.

For adaptability and tolerance, Table 3 gives 0.030/0.059 and 0.038/0.076 in columns headed “p one-factor” and “p two-factor”; the displayed intervals include zero. The article's significance claims require clarification of the intended tests and reporting. A reader should not silently choose the favorable column or manufacture a corrected test from unavailable participant data.

The current [mixed-research guidance](../../../skills/edu-writing/references/analysis-bridge.md) already asks what each source changes in the answer. This screened case reinforces the need to apply that question to the actual display and argument. It adds no mandatory structure, no new runtime rule and no writing-performance claim.
