# Writing rationale

## Predraft argument plan

This is a new, attributed English discussion passage of 450–550 body words, based exclusively on the supplied source pack. It is a secondary-writing demonstration, not an account of our own research. The task is a single section, so full-manuscript confirmation, a multi-section consistency check, reference-list preparation, and Word conversion do not apply.

**Question → evidence → contribution → scope.** How should researchers understand the relationship between mathematics self-concept and achievement? Across annual observations of Bavarian students from Grades 5 to 9, positive reciprocal lagged associations appeared for grades and standardized tests under both model families and the reported extensions. The contribution is a reciprocal account supported under distinct comparisons, with model interpretation and substantive paths considered alongside fit. It applies to these observational comparisons; mechanisms, causal intervention effects, benefit magnitudes, and effects for individual students remain unestablished.

**Evidence supporting the argument.** Both achievement indicators, both model families, and the reported lag-2/covariate extensions support the central directional pattern. The CLPM fit improvement alongside stable focal paths shows why a fit change need not overturn the substantive answer. The authors' complementary-model position and proposed factorial trial connect interpretation to subsequent research.

**Evidence constraining the argument.** Stable and within-person differences are not decomposed in the CLPM; the RI-CLPM estimates a common within-person parameter. Measured covariates offer only a weak confounding test, unmeasured time-varying confounding remains possible, and incomplete follow-up relies on missing-data assumptions. Feedback interpretation and motivational processes were not measured; the intervention combination was not tested. Exact coefficients are unresolved in the source pack.

**Paragraph functions.** Open with reciprocity and the added value of two achievement indicators; explain the two comparisons concretely; interpret specification changes and design limits; connect the candidate psychological process to attention to both beliefs and performance; end on the authors' factorial trial and the evidence it should supply. Paragraph allocation can change during the substantive revision.

**Terminology.** Use “mathematics self-concept” for beliefs about mathematical competence; “school grades” and “standardized test scores” for the distinct achievement measures; “cross-lagged panel model (CLPM)” and “random-intercept cross-lagged panel model (RI-CLPM)” at first use. Reserve “effects” for proposed intervention comparisons or the named reciprocal-effects account, and use “associations” or “predicted” for observations.

**Source boundary.** Every study claim will derive from source-pack.md. References to earlier evidence will be limited to the authors' reviewed conceptual alternatives. Do not cite the unread Haney and Durlak meta-analysis as independently verified evidence, infer coefficient magnitudes, or claim the original article or Mplus supplement was independently checked in this run.
