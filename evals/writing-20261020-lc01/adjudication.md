# L-C01 decision: retain the existing runtime guidance

The one-example addition does not demonstrate an advantage over the current qualitative route in this development comparison. Keep it as a source-linked development illustration; do not add it to the runtime skill. The current route already produces concrete interpretation and faithful compression on this material. This conclusion concerns this addition and study, not the value of learning from qualitative examples generally.

## What was actually compared

The control freezes the skill at `331eccc73459770dedad8c1f5f747929f69312f5`. The project baseline remains `684f5e435b76daf8d0109c9c6a93297542c59889`. The candidate appends only the L-N14 illustration to qualitative-narrative.md. All eight writers report reading their assigned qualitative route, and the reported resource hashes match the preserved files.

The test is L-N13, Unsworth's English ANT(LS) ethnography. Four runs write a 350–450-word subsection; four compress an actual 159-word published paragraph to 90–110 words. Each arm repeats each operation twice. This is one development study, not eight studies or an independent final holdout. The training illustration and test paper are distinct development sources. The writers and reviewers share the same model family; exact hosted configuration is not exposed.

The evidence boundary is important: reviewers read the frozen pack, not the original field data or the complete publications.

## Delivery context and reviewer qualifications

Attribution headings and editorial/source notes were intentionally withheld from both reviewers. They correctly avoid calling those components missing. The developer read the actual notes: all four compression writers say Colton was encountered through Unsworth and was not independently checked. Three retain the original citation form in the body; the first control uses an explicit secondary citation. Making the intermediary explicit can improve a standalone paragraph's transparency, but the delivered packages do not falsely claim independent access. Preserve this distinction rather than retroactively turning a conditional reviewer suggestion into a citation failure.

The source pack identifies the observations and the main text explains Google Classroom use for older pupils. The first-repeat compressions' school-level openings are less explicit than the second-repeat activity-level openings. Their contextual scope is still plausibly local. An added observational phrase is a precision option, not proof that those writers included every child in the school. A universal qualifier rule would exceed the evidence from this task.

## Process evidence and negatives

- The actual first drafts of both controls and candidate repeat 2 risked connecting the fruit-planning episode to the later number-line episode as one tracked document. Self-review separated the episodes while retaining the author's delegation account. This repair occurs across arms. It cannot be credited to the added example.
- The two control compressions produced intermediate 87- and 85-word drafts. Their failed length checks, drafts and subsequent repairs to 92 and 91 words are retained. Final compliance does not erase the earlier failures or make word count a quality measure.
- Attribution headings caused frequent automatic Introduction classification, and some checker cues treated opportunity could or observed limited speech as hedging or limitations. Writers adjudicated these cues semantically rather than deleting necessary content. All actual checker outputs remain available.
- Some initial PowerShell reads displayed incorrect encoding; the affected writers record rereading with UTF-8 before drafting. Frozen input bytes and final English bodies are intact. No new Chinese source or Chinese writing task is included.
- Unlike the preceding M-C01 batch, all reported resource/helper versions in this batch are available and hash-verified. This verifies the reports against bytes; it is not an independently instrumented trace of every read.

The developer read all eight first drafts, final bodies and self-reviews and both complete anonymous reviews. [Content comparison](content-comparison.md) shows actual before/after excerpts and the remaining ambiguity. Proposed fixes are not silently substituted into the experimental outputs.

## What this supports and what remains

The current version can, on this source, explain how concrete action gives a concept content and compress a real paragraph without replacing the author's position with generic caution. The added illustration does not supply a demonstrated extra decision worth loading by default. Keep the example and full results as development evidence, including the tie and local ambiguity; no further comparison of this unchanged addition is justified solely to search for a win.

The wider goal remains unfinished: the English corpus still needs broader coverage and synthesis, the other candidates have their own unresolved transfer questions, and the eventual actual runtime combination still needs a fresh-material independent check. This batch provides neither final-version validation nor evidence of reduced human editing time. No PPT, statistics, journal-search or review-annotation implementation changed.
