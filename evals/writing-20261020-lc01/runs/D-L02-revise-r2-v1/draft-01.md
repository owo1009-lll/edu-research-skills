# Secondary-writing exercise: Ruth Unsworth (2023), *Teaching through the cloud*

At Highland School, literacy practices in Google Classroom foregrounded typing and backgrounded speech: pupils largely entered their thoughts and ideas into online documents. Every pupil could participate, but communication was delegated to these documents, with little verbal interaction outside them. This pattern echoes Colton's (2019, p. 432) account of technology opening participation while human and nonhuman elements guide learners. The shared documents set the parameters of online discussion, positioning preplanned visuals as influential participants in classroom activity. Thus, the opportunity for all pupils to contribute coexisted with an organization of agency that favored those visuals.
