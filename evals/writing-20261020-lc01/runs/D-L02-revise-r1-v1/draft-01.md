# Attributed secondary-writing exercise based on Ruth Unsworth, *Teaching through the cloud* (2023 online-first), [doi:10.1111/aeq.12471](https://doi.org/10.1111/aeq.12471)

At Highland School, Google Classroom literacy practices foregrounded typing and backgrounded speech: students largely entered their thoughts and ideas into online documents, with little spoken interaction outside them. Every student could contribute, but communication was delegated to documents that set the parameters of discussion. This pattern echoes Colton's (2019, as cited in Unsworth, 2023, p. 14) account of participation opened through technology yet organized by human and nonhuman elements. Agency was thus balanced in favor of preplanned cloud-based visuals, which structured what students could discuss and how they could participate.
