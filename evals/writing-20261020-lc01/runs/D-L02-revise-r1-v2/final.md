# Attributed secondary-writing exercise: Ruth Unsworth, *Teaching through the cloud* (2023)

At Highland School, literacy practices in Google Classroom foregrounded typing over speech: pupils largely entered their thoughts and ideas into shared documents, with little verbal interaction outside these spaces. Every pupil could contribute to online discussions, yet communication was delegated to the documents in which they typed. This echoes Colton's (2019, p. 432) account of educational technology opening participation while human and nonhuman elements guide pupils' activity. The documents therefore enabled participation while setting its parameters; classroom agency was balanced in favor of the preplanned visuals that framed those discussions.
