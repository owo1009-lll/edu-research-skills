# Attributed secondary-writing exercise: Ruth Unsworth, *Teaching through the cloud* (2023)

At Highland School, literacy practices in Google Classroom foregrounded typing over speech: pupils largely entered their thoughts and ideas into shared documents, with little verbal interaction outside these spaces. Every pupil could contribute, but communication was delegated to documents that set the parameters of discussion. This echoes Colton's (2019, p. 432) account of educational technology opening participation while human and nonhuman elements guide students' activity. Participation and its organization thus occurred together: shared documents offered pupils a voice while preplanned visuals structured their contributions. Classroom agency was balanced in favor of those visuals.
