# Actual English revisions and remaining editorial work

These comparisons use the saved manuscripts, not reconstructed before/after drafts. The source is Unsworth's public English ethnography. They illustrate actual editorial changes during the experiment; they do not by themselves show that the candidate improved writing.

## Keep distinct episodes distinct while developing an explanation

The first control run initially moved from fruit planning to the number-line lesson with:

> Shared documents also carried these agreements across classroom boundaries.

Its final version makes the evidentiary relationship explicit:

> A separate early-years episode shows how shared documents carried planning agreements into instruction.

The earlier referent could imply that the fruit activity was subsequently observed in the number-line classroom. The final transition preserves delegation as the author's explanatory concept while distinguishing the observations. Both control repeats and candidate repeat 2 identified this kind of problem during their actual self-reviews. It is therefore evidence that the current workflow can repair it, not an improvement unique to the candidate.

See [control first draft](runs/D-L02-new-r1-v1/draft-01.md), [final](runs/D-L02-new-r1-v1/final.md) and [self-review](runs/D-L02-new-r1-v1/self-review.md).

## Give the conceptual claim content

The same control final explains the fruit edits with:

> The slide gave proposals a visible form that colleagues could encounter and revise as their agreement developed.

The candidate's first-repeat final also gives the conceptual point concrete content:

> Talk, images and visible edits jointly settled the proposed activity and who would perform it.

Both do more than label the episode collaboration or documentary agency. They explain how the medium participates in agreement through visible proposals and revisions. The blind new-writing review rates all four bodies in the same strong tier; these passages do not justify a general superiority claim. See [candidate final](runs/D-L02-new-r1-v2/final.md).

## A smooth connection can still need clarification

Candidate repeat 2 concludes part of its final paragraph:

> Images that helped teachers negotiate lesson content thus also framed the space in which pupils' ideas were expressed and discussed.

The blind reviewer notes that the sentence can mean images as a category, but may also imply that the identical images were followed across separate episodes. A local clarification could distinguish the planning images from the classroom visuals. The original final is retained; the reviewer's proposed repair is in [the actual review](reviews/new/review.md), not silently substituted into the experimental text. This is an ambiguity, not an established invented observation.

## Compress the actual argument rather than replace it

The [159-word published original](inputs/D-L02/original.md) connects typed interaction, opportunity to contribute, delegation to documents, a literature comparison and agency favoring preplanned visuals. The actual final compressions are:

| Run | Body words | Retained relationships and distinct choice |
|---|---:|---|
| [Control, repeat 1](runs/D-L02-revise-r1-v1/final.md) | 92 | Typing and limited speech, opportunity, delegation, discussion parameters, the Colton comparison and the agency judgment; explicit secondary citation in the body |
| [Candidate, repeat 1](runs/D-L02-revise-r1-v2/final.md) | 90 | The same core relationships; inherited citation retained, with its unverified status in separate notes |
| [Control, repeat 2](runs/D-L02-revise-r2-v1/final.md) | 91 | The same core relationships; opening explicitly scoped to observed Google Classroom activities |
| [Candidate, repeat 2](runs/D-L02-revise-r2-v2/final.md) | 92 | The same core relationships; opening scoped to Google Classroom activities and closing connects having a say with what can be discussed |

The two control runs briefly produced 87- and 85-word intermediate revisions and failed their length check, then restored sufficient wording. Those actual drafts, failed outputs and later corrections remain in the archive. The final lengths satisfy the request; length compliance does not establish writing quality.

All four retain an affirmative qualitative interpretation. They do not substitute generic caution for the agency claim. The final candidate repeat 2 also keeps two uses of could in its closing sentence after the checker flags them: they describe participation opportunities, not uncertainty about the author's interpretation. That judgment is explained in its [self-review](runs/D-L02-revise-r2-v2/self-review.md). No keyword threshold determines whether to keep the words.

## Source access and presentation

All four compression writers record that Colton's original work was not independently read. The first control makes the secondary access explicit in the paragraph; the other three preserve the source's inherited citation and disclose access in separate notes. The task permits editing the supplied paragraph, and the current foundation guidance distinguishes inherited unverified citations from verified originals. A body-only preference for explicit secondary attribution should not be turned into a false claim that the delivered package concealed its reading scope.

The checkers often classify an attribution-led body as an Introduction. Writers retain the actual outputs and adjudicate the irrelevant citation/self-mention cues against this bounded task. Successful script exits are not visual or scholarly validation. There is no slide rendering or trial speaking-time claim in this writing experiment.
