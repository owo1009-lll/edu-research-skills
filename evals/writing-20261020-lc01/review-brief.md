# Independent anonymous review brief

Review the four English manuscripts A–D for your assigned operation using only this folder's request, source-pack.md and, for compression, original.md. Do not browse, inspect other runs, keys, candidate instructions or other reviews. Do not spawn agents or edit manuscripts.

These are body-only copies. Attribution headings and accompanying editorial/source notes are intentionally withheld. Their existence and integrity are checked separately; do not infer missing delivery components from their absence here.

Assess every manuscript on nine dimensions:

1. Factual and authorial fidelity.
2. Research problem and central judgment.
3. Reasoning and evidence organization.
4. Theory and literature use, including secondary attribution.
5. Explanation and concrete contribution.
6. Naturalness, specificity and rhythm.
7. Authorial voice, conceptual framework and justified strength.
8. Necessary qualification and avoidable repetition.
9. Concrete corrections an author would still need to make.

Support judgments with specific passages. Distinguish substantive errors from optional editorial choices. Neither confidence nor caution is automatically better; no keyword counts, sentence-length targets or AI detectors. Assess the explanatory work done by the selected material without requiring every source-pack scene or a fixed paragraph structure. For compression, assess retention of the original paragraph's argument and concepts as well as justified repairs.

Save English review.md, review.json and input-hashes.json (each input path and SHA-256) in your assigned review output directory. Include a comparative judgment with ties where appropriate, remaining corrections and uncertainty. Do not infer the arms or force a winner. Preserve the actual review without rewriting it after any key is disclosed. This is an AI review, not human or cross-model validation.
