The test source is Unsworth's public English classroom ethnography, DOI [10.1111/aeq.12471](https://doi.org/10.1111/aeq.12471); the illustration uses a different English development source, Fillmore, Phyak and Paudel, DOI [10.1007/s11159-025-10217-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11159-025-10217-7). No private materials or new Chinese tasks are included. The two repetitions concern one test study, not independent research settings.

The four new subsections contain 391–407 body words. The actual 159-word source paragraph becomes four 90–92-word final paragraphs. Two intermediate control revisions were too short and are retained with their corrections. One candidate new-writing final has a minor ambiguity about images across separate episodes. All variants preserve the central qualitative interpretation; no score or keyword count decides adoption.

Reviewers share the writers' model family. They assess the frozen source pack and anonymized bodies; the developer checks full delivery records separately. This is development evidence, not a final independent holdout, human review or measured editing-time result. The broader project remains in progress.
