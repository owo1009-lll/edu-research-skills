## First draft to final

- Replaced the first draft's “financial measure” with “binary food-security classification.” The initial wording incorrectly narrowed the instrument to finances; the source itself did not make that error.
- Added April 2021, 2,116 complete responses and 176 written statements to anchor the mixed-methods interpretation. Kept the prevalence figures scoped to undergraduate and graduate respondent groups rather than assigning the full-response denominator to them.
- Replaced “was reported by” with “were classified as having” for the prevalence estimates and explicitly defined the combined category.
- Removed the repeated directionality clause from the academic-support paragraph; its meaning remains in the integrated limitations paragraph.
- Refined “what recipients … can prepare” to “what recipients … know how to prepare,” matching the voucher account's practical concern more closely.
- Reframed the opening and ending around connecting material and practical access needs. Recommendations retain clear institutional actors and “should” where supported.

## Deliberate omissions for length

Community-garden startup detail, emergency grant amounts, work-study wage-sharing rules, app/staffing specifics, relief-payment timing, the full demographic overrepresentation inventory and subgroup degree comparisons are omitted. No omitted element is replaced by an unsupported generalization. The core action families, practical barriers, local prevalence, one precisely presented housing association, variation in assistance experience and principal design limits remain.
