# Writing rationale and source record

## Task and main line (prepared before first.md)

This is a new, bounded English secondary writing exercise, not a revision of an author manuscript or a claim to have conducted the published study. Deliver only the requested Introduction, Discussion and Conclusion, targeting 600-750 words excluding references. The opening notice identifies the original study. Markdown is explicitly requested; no Word conversion is needed. No target journal is specified and no outside search is authorized.

**Educational decision:** whether to allocate initial encounters with new course content to a deliberately structured AI tutor when an established active-learning class is available.

**What the evidence answers:** a randomized crossover comparison in Harvard introductory physics favored the AI instructional package on immediate topic quizzes. Common content and objectives and experienced classroom teachers make the contrast educationally meaningful. The experiment compares a package: expert-authored steps, feedback, sequencing, a recorded introduction, individual home study and pacing all differ together.

**Core judgment and contribution:** designed AI tutoring can improve immediate initial learning relative to a substantial active-learning comparator in this setting. The educational contribution is a concrete option for organizing initial learning and an implementation-sensitive interpretation of AI evidence. No component mechanism, general superiority across education, durable learning advantage or complete blended-course benefit is established.

**Scope and action:** a pilot of similarly designed initial-learning activities is warranted where expert preparation is feasible; measure retained understanding, complex transfer, classroom integration, costs and access before extending the claim to a course or institution.

## Evidence supporting and constraining the judgment

Supporting: randomized crossover; common content/objectives; experienced and highly rated instructors; higher median immediate post-test score; adjusted standardized coefficient 0.63; estimate retained under peer-group clustering; higher reported engagement and motivation; brief logged tutoring sessions.

Constraining: one course and two topics; immediate quizzes; bundled teaching conditions; no manipulation isolating feedback or pacing; unequal bases for timing; single-item experience measures; no detected difference on enjoyment or confidence with effort; considerable preparation; no tests of retention, complex synthesis, equitable access or costs. Sample accounting and experience-test reporting questions remain in work records below.
