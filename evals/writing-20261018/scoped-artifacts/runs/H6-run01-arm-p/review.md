State what that comparison permits: distinguishing the educational tasks and support provided by implementations. Keep Kestin et al. as the explicitly read-through source. No missing bibliographic entries for Krupp or Forero should be invented.

Directly state the positive scores and logged duration, then identify that classroom duration is assumed. Recommend comparable measurement rather than treating the reported contrast as an efficiency estimate.
4. **Separate the teaching decision from overall scope.** One paragraph should justify a pilot from the learning result, state the expert-preparation condition, and describe collaborative class work as a proposed application. A following paragraph should explain what single-course, immediate assessment leaves open and specify outcomes and implementation questions for evaluation. This is a reasoning repair, not an added checklist.
