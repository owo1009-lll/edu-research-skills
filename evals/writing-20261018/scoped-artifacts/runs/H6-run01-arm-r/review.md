# AI self-review of first.md

This is an AI self-review, not external peer review or independent verification of the published article. The complete first draft and all three saved first-draft check outputs were inspected. Review scope is the supplied request, factual packet and three requested sections.

## Overall assessment

The first draft meets the requested length (646 words in the section bodies, counted as whitespace-separated words; headings, provenance notice and reference excluded). It asks a clear teaching question, describes a credible classroom comparator, retains the randomized peer-group crossover structure, answers in the Discussion and Conclusion, and labels the piece as secondary writing. It has no fabricated citation or new analysis. However, the central educational interpretation could be more precise and accessible: the coefficient and clustering sentence occupy space without telling a nonspecialist what was observed, while the closing judgement repeats the general idea of a designed arrangement.

## Manual factual audit of the first draft

- 0.63 refers to the adjusted standardized AI coefficient, not a mean difference, confidence interval or Cohen's d. The extraction artifact is resolved in the supplied material.
- 49 is the tutor activity-log median in minutes; 60 is assumed classroom instructional time. The draft identifies this asymmetry.
- The crossover spans two consecutive weeks and two physics topics; randomization is by existing peer groups, not by independent score observations.
- Experienced staff and common worksheets support the meaningful comparison. They do not isolate one component.
- Immediate topic assessment is preserved in the main answer and conclusion. No retention, whole-course achievement or causal mechanism is invented.
- Lower-level account of learner perceptions is not an accuracy audit. No explanation-quality percentage or subgroup-equality claim is included.
- The only bibliography entry is the source supplied in the packet. Its DOI and author details were checked against that packet, not online.

## Cross-section judgement before revision

The Introduction asks about immediate initial learning relative to an active-learning class; the Discussion answers this in the affirmative for the tested package; the Conclusion retains the immediate-topic scope. Candidate mechanisms occur only in Discussion. Combined tutoring and classroom collaboration is consistently proposed rather than reported as tested. Revision will make the practical consequence of this bounded answer clearer, not change the core result.
