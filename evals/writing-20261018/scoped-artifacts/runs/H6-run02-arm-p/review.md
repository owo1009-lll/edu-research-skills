## Revisions required before delivery

1. **Narrow the opening decision.** Whether AI belongs in teaching is broader than this experiment. Start with guided initial learning relative to an active class, then finish the Introduction with the immediate topic-learning question. The Conclusion should answer it while retaining the course scope.
2. **Explain the comparator's importance.** Connect common objectives and experienced instructors to the educational relevance of the advantage. Remove the rhetorical comparison with an unsupported control, which adds no evidence and may imply knowledge of other studies' controls.
3. **Identify the adjusted estimate.** Retain 0.63 as a standardized regression coefficient, accounting for starting performance and other measured differences. Do not call it Cohen's d, apply a generic effect-size band or invent an interval. The unavailable interval stays a work-record query.
4. **Connect explanation and design.** Link feedback and flexible pacing to the tutor's actual arrangements, then explain that setting, recording and peer interaction also changed. Candidate explanations remain unresolved. A component comparison is a future design, not an analysis conducted here. Keep earlier-study comparisons explicitly attributed to Kestin et al.'s review.
5. **Tighten the time interpretation.** The first draft's 'promising relationship between learning and lesson time' could invite a learning-per-minute claim. State the logged-versus-assumed distinction and explain that quantifying efficiency requires comparable measures. Do not derive a saving percentage or a pacing mechanism.
6. **Preserve mixed student-experience results.** Engagement and motivation have higher reported item scores; enjoyment and confidence have no statistically detected differences. Retain single-item status and avoid equivalence. Omit the disputed test label/degrees of freedom rather than silently repairing them. Keep the query in rationale.md.
7. **Make the application concrete.** Tie evaluation of tutoring to capacity for expert content, sequencing and feedback. Preparation days and platform-development months make resources tangible. Present tutoring followed by collaborative class work as an arrangement to evaluate, because the whole-course combination was not tested.
8. **Improve connected prose without chasing corpus averages.** Join closely related evidence and interpretation where useful. Preserve the three sections and limited source base. Consolidate general scope in the last Discussion paragraph, followed by a specific conclusion.
