Generative AI offers rapid and personalized feedback and can therefore improve undergraduate writing. Few studies have examined AI-assisted peer feedback in our local context. We plan to investigate students' experiences of AI-assisted peer feedback and their use of suggestions during argumentative essay revision. We argue that students should retain responsibility for judging feedback, and that feedback provision should be evaluated through what students do with it and how their writing changes.
