# Rationale prepared before the first draft

Question: How do women in physics and physical sciences construct science identities in relation to gender? Educational stake: participation and enthusiasm may coexist with exclusion from scientific communities.

Evidence: Eren (2021), supplied introduction, theoretical framework, design, methodological limitations and findings; interviews with 29 women across four Dublin universities, undergraduate through postdoctoral levels. Published discursive narrative and thematic interpretations are the evidence; no independent coding was performed.

Main line: distinguish emotional attachment from community attachment, then explain how participants revise and sometimes reproduce gendered scientific identities. Contribution: scientific identification can persist alongside exclusion, and agency does not require rejection of femininity. Scope: situated accounts, including retrospective accounts of change; cross-sectional interviews do not establish a developmental sequence.
