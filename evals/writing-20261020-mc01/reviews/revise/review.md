**What the supplied evidence supports**

The original paragraph explains generally higher self-ratings through possible knowledge limitations and teachers' allocation of responsibility, then calls for research on teachers' and observers' perspectives. A compression should retain that explanatory task, not merely catalogue limitations.

The survey-observation difference supplies the phenomenon to explain, but typical practice across five elements and one selected lesson across four elements are not equivalent assessments. The interviews supply a possible meaning-based explanation: teachers may judge their own provision favourably when they locate responsibility for cooperation in students. They cannot establish that the same teachers gave high self-ratings, because those individual self-reported implementation scores are not displayed. All four use this relationship productively, with different levels of explicitness.

The relevant conceptual distinction is between fostering cooperation as part of teaching and treating students' capacity to cooperate as a prerequisite. Distinguishing instructional provision from actual student cooperation is also legitimate; the explanation should identify how teachers interpret responsibility rather than imply that the distinction itself is necessarily mistaken. A five-element theoretical inventory is unnecessary in a paragraph this short.

Each body retains the original paragraph's statement that Hennessey and Dionigi (2013) emphasise knowledge in assessing CL. The supplied pack says this is the published authors' reviewed citation and that the cited original was not independently read. Retention in an attributed compression is not evidence of independent verification. Because attribution and source-check notes were withheld, no omission of those components is inferred and no candidate is penalized for their absence.

Omission of the malformed correlation, the inconsistent partial-implementation total, scale-bound issues, recruitment details and general study limitations is appropriate here: none is needed for the retained claims, and adding them would displace the paragraph's task.
