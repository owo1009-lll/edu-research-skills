# Independent anonymous review brief

Review the four English manuscripts A–D for the operation assigned to you. Read only the supplied anonymous manuscripts, the task request, source-pack.md and, for compression, original.md. Do not browse, inspect the working repository, other runs, keys, candidate instructions or other reviews. Do not spawn agents or edit manuscripts.

The manuscripts are body-only copies. The separate attribution heading and accompanying editorial/source-check notes have intentionally been withheld to anonymize the writing. Their existence and integrity are checked separately. Do not infer that a writer failed to deliver those components. Evaluate the body against the actual task and supplied evidence.

Assess each manuscript on all nine dimensions:

1. Factual and authorial fidelity.
2. The research problem and central judgment.
3. Reasoning and organization of evidence.
4. Theory and literature use, including attribution of sources not independently read.
5. Explanation and concrete contribution.
6. Naturalness, specificity and rhythm.
7. Authorial voice, conceptual framework and justified strength of judgment.
8. Necessary qualifications and avoidable repetition.
9. Concrete corrections an author would still need to make.

Use passage-specific evidence. Distinguish substantive errors from optional editorial preferences. Neither stronger language nor greater caution is automatically better. Do not use keyword counts, sentence length or an AI detector as a quality proxy. Consider what the two sources jointly add, the actual correspondence between them and the study's substantive position, without requiring a fixed integration sentence or structure. For compression, assess retention of the original paragraph's task and concepts as well as any justified repair.

Save an English review.md and review.json in the assigned review output directory. Include a comparative judgment with ties when appropriate, concrete remaining corrections and uncertainty. Do not infer the experimental arms or force a winning manuscript. Preserve the actual review; do not rewrite it after learning any key. Record every input path and SHA-256 in input-hashes.json. No human-review or cross-model claim is warranted by this pass.
