## Original argument map, recorded before drafting

1. Teacher self-reports and observations differ in their assessment of CL implementation quality.
2. Limited knowledge of CL's basic elements is a possible explanation. The authors associate this possibility with limited training and cite Hennessey and Dionigi (2013) on knowledge and assessment.
3. Interviews offer a further explanation: attributing responsibility for cooperation to students may allow teachers to separate their own implementation from students' cooperation when judging success.
4. The published paragraph then asserts that the interviewed teachers rated their implementation highly despite poor student engagement. The source pack identifies missing individual implementation-rating correspondence, so that asserted result requires repair.
5. The paragraph closes by advocating further examination of teacher and observer perspectives. This purpose should remain central; the compression should not become a broader discussion of training or beliefs across the study.
