## Supporting and constraining evidence

Supporting evidence: the reported source discrepancy; the existing knowledge citation; interview accounts assigning cooperation to students. The educational recognition is that judging instructional provision may depend on what teachers see as their responsibility when students cooperate poorly.

Constraining evidence: training attendance is not a knowledge measure; the four interview cases have no displayed individual implementation self-ratings; the questionnaire and observation differ in timeframe and element coverage. The paragraph will not claim that a belief caused a rating or that the measurement differences caused the source discrepancy.
