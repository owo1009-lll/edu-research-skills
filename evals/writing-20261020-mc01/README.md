# English mixed-methods comparison: M-C01

Eight completed English manuscripts compare the current edu-writing skill with one added positive integration example. New-writing quality was substantively tied. The compression review slightly preferred both candidate outputs, but remaining ambiguities and the single-study scope do not justify runtime adoption. See the [adjudication](adjudication.md) for passages, disagreements and the decision.

One public cooperative-learning study supplies two operations, with two repetitions per operation and arm. New discussions require 350–450 words; compression uses an actual 176-word published paragraph and requires 120–145 words. Repeated runs on this article are not independent research situations or final holdouts. All manuscripts, writer notes and reviews are English; historical Chinese materials were not used.
