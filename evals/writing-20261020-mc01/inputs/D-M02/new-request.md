# English discussion task

Write a 350–450-word English discussion passage based on the accompanying source-pack.md. The audience is education researchers and teacher educators who know the idea of cooperative learning but have not read this study. Explain what the study adds to understanding its classroom implementation and the implications for teacher education. Use connected prose without internal subheadings; make a defensible argument rather than an inventory of results.

Use only the supplied material. Do not browse or introduce additional literature. Attribute this as a secondary-writing demonstration based on Adl-Amini, Völlinger, and Eckart; do not present their study as your own. Keep the attribution heading outside the word count. Deliver Markdown only, plus a short separate note explaining the writing choices and any unresolved source checks.
