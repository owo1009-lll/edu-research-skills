## What the actual passages do

All four new discussions identify a specific educational contribution. Observations show variation in instructional provision; the selected interviews distinguish treating cooperation as a competence to teach from treating social skills as a prerequisite. The case in which academic success coexists with groups not working together gives content to teachers' criteria for success. Recommendations address planning, responses to communication difficulties and lesson appraisal. They retain should where it expresses a reasoned practical judgment, while distinguishing those recommendations from tested training effects.

The candidate r2 passage makes the omitted communication planning especially concrete. Control r1 is especially explicit that observation scores selected interviews already conducted after lessons. Control r2 states the competence/prerequisite distinction directly. Candidate r1 connects the assessed arrangements and application economically. These strengths occur in both arms. All four use four paragraphs with a scope paragraph before implications; this recurring structure is an observed output pattern, not evidence that the example increases structural flexibility or a format to prescribe.

All four compressions retain the original explanatory task, existing citation and research purpose. The published paragraph's asserted individual correspondence becomes a possible interpretation with a stated evidence gap. Measurement periods and coverage are made explicit. No compression substitutes a new whole-study discussion for the authors' paragraph.

The compression reviewer reasonably favors the candidates' explicit responsibility-to-self-assessment link. Both candidates nevertheless say **individual implementation ratings** where the absent data are specifically **self-reported implementation ratings**. The surrounding wording makes the intended meaning recoverable, but observed ratings did exist and guided case selection. Control r1 and r2 explicitly identify self-ratings. This candidate imprecision remains in the archived output; no post-review rewrite is substituted for the evaluated version.

The reviewer also favors candidate r1's express distinction between training attendance and knowledge, and candidate r2's omission of the training fact. Root qualification: control r1 already presents knowledge as a possible explanation. Its adjacent training fact is context, not an explicit claim that attendance measures knowledge. Making that relationship clearer is useful, but a mandatory disclaimer in every such paragraph would overstate the demonstrated defect. Candidate r2's omission is a defensible compression choice; it is not proof that deleting the context is always better. This is one reason not to convert the narrow preference into a new universal requirement.

## Process checks and limitations

The source pack deliberately supplies checked facts and unresolved source issues. This tests writing from those facts, not unaided discovery of the original paper's errors. Neither writers nor reviewers independently read Hennessey and Dionigi (2013); all compression notes retain that as a source check. Their inherited citation is not presented as newly verified.

Actual voice/prose checker outputs include low-citation and self-mention prompts inappropriate to a source-limited secondary-writing task, heading-recognition failures, and cues that misclassify a limited-knowledge explanation as a generic limitation. Writers retained the outputs and used body-identical checking copies when needed. Successful process exit and zero prompts are not quality certification. The candidate r2 compression runner also recorded an initial regex failure before either checker ran, then a successful retry; no prose stage was reconstructed from that failure.

The frozen original's appended bibliographic context contains an encoding defect documented in [input-context-correction.md](input-context-correction.md). The published paragraph itself matches the source extraction exactly; all writers received the same frozen inputs, the source pack has correct metadata, and none of the eight final headings/bodies propagates the defective name/title/pagination. The defect and shared preparation conditions still limit the experiment; no claim of absolutely zero influence is made.

The first archive attempt stopped at a hash mismatch for control compression r2's own audit.py. Its resource log contains both an earlier and later execution digest, but only the later helper file remains. The earlier bytes are not reconstructed or marked verified. The archive preserves the log and lists this single [historical helper gap](historical-helper-gaps.json) separately from 117 matching resource entries. The exception is restricted to that exact run-created Python helper and both recorded digests; frozen skills and task inputs remain strict checks. The records therefore support verification of the actual writing instructions and outputs, but not complete reconstruction of every transient helper version.

The current skill already provides much of the tested capability. The positive example remains plausible and may help in another integration task, but this batch does not establish its additional value across designs, authors or studies. Final runtime selection and independent review on new material remain outstanding. No changes were made to PPT, statistics, journal retrieval or native annotation functionality in this batch, and their tests were not repeated for these development-only edits.
