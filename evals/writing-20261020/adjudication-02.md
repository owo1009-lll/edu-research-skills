## Checking the reviewers' reservations

- The quantitative review could not inspect the separate explanation and adaptation record. Both exist: [new-writing rationale](runs/D-Q01-new-run01-current/rationale.md) distinguishes the supplied summary from independent source reading; [revision rationale](runs/D-Q01-revise-run01-current/rationale.md) identifies the published source, licence, adaptation and unverified retained citation. The same delivery components exist for [qualitative new writing](runs/D-L01-new-run01-current/rationale.md) and [compression](runs/D-L01-revise-run01-current/rationale.md). They were excluded from blinded manuscript review by design, so their absence from that review is not an omitted deliverable.
- “Shared gain” in the quantitative opening could name achievement and wellbeing explicitly. The surrounding text already identifies both, so this remains a local clarity preference, not a demonstrated inference error or a reason to weaken the policy recommendation.
- In qualitative new writing, “a more informative guide” is framed as an interpretation of accounts, not a measured superiority result. Replacing it with “suggest judging” is optional. The two uses of “revisit” concern evolving arrangements and capacity constraints; a shorter alternative must preserve both meanings. The review does not establish a need for a repeated-word rule.
- In qualitative compression, “a usual precursor” could be made more idiomatic without changing its conditional force. The omitted citation [43] accompanies an omitted, grammatically unclear research-centres clause in the original. The writer explains that choice separately and retains [31, 42] with the surviving workforce claim. There is no evidence here that [43] is erroneous or that a retained claim lost its citation.
- Retained citations were inherited from the published excerpts. Neither writer nor reviewer read the cited originals. Faithfulness to those excerpts is not independent validation of the underlying literature.

## First drafts and workflow failures remain visible

The qualitative new-writing draft described contextual knowledge as “often required” where the pack supported its value, and described policy-makers as unable to “sustain” co-production where the source only said they had not found it possible. The [saved self-review](runs/D-L01-new-run01-current/self-review.md) identifies both distinctions and the final corrects them. This is evidence about the complete draft–review workflow, not proof that the initial generation is reliable.

The qualitative compression self-review restores the likelihood-of-impact distinction, participant attribution and “always considered essential,” rather than making every judgment weaker. Two below-minimum drafts are retained with the [actual counts](runs/D-L01-revise-run01-current/word-counts.json). The quantitative compression run also retains a 158-word intermediate version and character damage from a shell pipe, followed by the corrected final and [format check](runs/D-Q01-revise-run01-current/final-format-check.json).

The quantitative new-writing run preserves initial checker outputs affected by a UTF-8 byte-order mark and the retries after removing it. Its prose checker also failed to recognise the accented surname in the narrative citation. The writer did not add unnecessary references to satisfy that prompt. These are recorded checker/workflow limits; this batch does not claim those scripts were fixed.

Three writers ran the skill's voice/prose scripts on their first drafts; these scripts were not rerun on those finals. The qualitative compression writer used local semantic review and length checks without skill scripts. All four finals received separate independent manuscript review. Script exit codes, word counts and load hashes do not establish writing quality or original-source factual accuracy.
