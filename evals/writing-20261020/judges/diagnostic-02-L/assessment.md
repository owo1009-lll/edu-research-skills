Paragraph 1, sentence 4 supplies the first judgment; paragraph 2, sentences 1 and 4 extend it into decisions about involvement. This answers what the study contributes to understanding partnerships without reducing the parties to opposing professional incentives. The opening says the study "sharpens our understanding" rather than claiming that the distinction between initiation and collaboration was discovered without prompting.
