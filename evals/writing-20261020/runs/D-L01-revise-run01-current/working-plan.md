# Local revision plan

This is an attributed adaptation of Williamson et al. (2019), Discussion paragraph 2 (locator B070), for an editing exercise. The source is health and social policy research; it is not an education intervention or the present writer's study.

The original argument proceeds from disagreement over co-production as an ideal, to the common requirement to agree aims, goals and deliverables, to task-dependent collaboration, and then to constraints on enacting the ideal. Some participants accepted largely independent researcher work after agreement; evaluation and implementation were considered especially suited to co-production because of policy-makers' tacit, experiential knowledge. The practical position is selective collaboration, with trusting relationships and workforce stability supporting feasibility.
